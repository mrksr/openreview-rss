We look forward to your presentation at the upcoming RSS2025! This email contains important information that can help you prepare for your poster and spotlight talk. 

**Setting Up Your Poster**

For your poster presentation, please check the following page to locate your poster: https://roboticsconference.org/program/posters/. Also recall the instructions on how and when to set up your poster here: https://roboticsconference.org/information/presentationInstructions/.  You can use the easel for the entire day of your poster presentation. 

**Before Your Talk**
- **Arrive Early:** Please arrive at the backstage area at least 25 minutes before your scheduled talk time. Consult this map for instructions on how to reach the backstage from both inside and outside the Bovard auditorium: https://roboticsconference.org/images/local2025/MapsToBovardStageDoor.pdf 
- **Mic Up & Prep:** While backstage, our team will help you get hooked up with your microphone and ensure you are ready to step onto the stage.
- **Amenities:** Restrooms and water will be available backstage, but we highly recommend you come prepared and well-hydrated.

**During Your Talk**
- **Introduction:** Once your slot is up, the session chair will introduce you. While the session chair is making the introduction, you will be walking from backstage onto the stage.
- **Presentation:** After the session chair has finished introducing you, please begin your presentation immediately. A timer and your presentation playback will start when you start speaking. 
- **Strict Timing:** Your presentation is allocated 4.5 minutes, strictly enforced.
- **Q&A:** Following your presentation, there will be 1.5 minutes for Q&A.
- **Visible Timer:** For both your presentation and the Q&A, you will see a countdown timer visible to you across the stage, helping you manage your time effectively. The timer also features a color indicator; an initially green light during the presentation and Q&A will turn yellow once there are 30 seconds left, and then red when time is up. When the red light shows, you need to end the presentation and move on to Q&A, or end Q&A and exit the stage.
- **Flexibility on Stage:** Feel free to present from the podium, the middle of the stage, or anywhere else you feel most comfortable, but make sure you do not drop the microphone by moving too abruptly.

**After Your Talk**
- **Exit Promptly:** Once the Q&A concludes, please exit the stage from the opposite side from which you entered.
- **On-Site Guidance:** We will remind everyone of these procedures during the conference to ensure clarity.

Your timely arrival backstage is crucial for a seamless transition between speakers. 

Thank you for your cooperation, and we look forward to your presentation!

Best regards,
The RSS 2025 Organizing Committee
